DMCA Policy

We respect intellectual property rights and respond promptly to legitimate DMCA notices.

Our Commitment

YouTube-Thumbnail-Downloader.cc respects the intellectual property rights of creators and copyright holders. It is our policy to respond to clear notices of alleged copyright infringement that comply with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA).

Important Note About Our Service

Our tool does not host, store, or distribute YouTube thumbnail images. We provide a browser-based interface that retrieves publicly accessible images directly from YouTube's own CDN servers. We do not cache, copy, or reproduce any thumbnail images on our servers. Therefore, DMCA takedown requests related to the content of specific thumbnails should properly be directed to YouTube directly at: YouTube's Copyright Center.

What We Can Address

If you believe our website itself (not YouTube's content) infringes your copyright — such as written content on our blog, images we use in our own UI, or other original content — we will promptly investigate and respond to valid DMCA notices.

Filing a DMCA Notice

To file a valid DMCA notice regarding content on our website, your notice must include: your physical or electronic signature; identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed; identification of the allegedly infringing material on our site; your contact information; a statement of good faith belief that the use is not authorized; and a statement under penalty of perjury that the information is accurate and you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.

Send DMCA notices to: dmca@youtube-thumbnail-downloader.cc

Counter-Notices

If you believe content was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification, you may file a counter-notice. We will restore the content within 10–14 business days unless the notifying party seeks a court order.
