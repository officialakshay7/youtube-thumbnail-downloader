How It Works

Downloading a YouTube thumbnail takes less than 10 seconds. Here's exactly how to do it on any device.

Step by Step

Three Simple Steps

1

Find Your YouTube Video

Open YouTube and navigate to the video whose thumbnail you want to download. It can be any public video — regular, a Short, from any channel worldwide.

2

Copy the Video URL

Copy the URL from your browser's address bar. You can also tap the Share button under the video and copy the link from there. Both formats work perfectly.

3

Paste, Download, Done

Paste the URL into our tool on the homepage and click Download. All available thumbnail resolutions will appear instantly. Click your preferred size to save it.

Frequently Asked "How To" Questions

How to download on iPhone?

On iPhone, after clicking the Download button, the image may open in a new tab (Safari behavior). Simply long-press the image and tap "Add to Photos" or "Save to Files" to keep it.

How to download on Android?

On Android, the download should trigger automatically to your Downloads folder. If it opens in a new tab instead, long-press the image and tap "Download Image" or "Save Image".

How to get the highest quality thumbnail?

Always look for the "MAX HD" option first. This is YouTube's highest resolution thumbnail (1280×720). If it's not available for a video, try "SD" which is the next best option at 640×480.
