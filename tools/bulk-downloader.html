How to Use the Bulk Thumbnail Downloader

  1. Paste your YouTube URLs into the text area above — one URL per line.
  2. You can enter up to 10 YouTube video URLs at once.
  3. Click "Download All Thumbnails" to process all URLs simultaneously.
  4. Each video's thumbnails will appear with download buttons for each quality.
